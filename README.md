# Five Nights at Freddy's Takeover

**Minecraft** × Five Nights at Freddy's · visual · idea by the team

Bring Five Nights at Freddy's into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I dropped Freddy's pizzeria into Minecraft: the animatronics freeze when you stare and sprint when you blink. Foxy got a sword to the face.

## Install

Copy the mod jar into your Minecraft Fabric mods folder (Fabric Loader and Fabric API required) and start a world. A pizzeria appears around you and the night shift begins: watch the animatronics, close the office door, and survive until 6 AM.
